# Storylines — MCU Bridge launch (16:9)

**The one thing:** your AI can now flash the real board on your desk. **To remember:** "I told my AI to blink the LED and the LED blinked."
**One viewer, one task:** a maker at 11:42 pm with a demo at 9. The real datum that travels the whole film: the sentence *"Flash blink to Desk ESP32"* → a compiled image → the board's LED.

## A — "11:42 pm" (recommended, built)
- **World / device / form:** a cutting-mat workbench seen from above · a moment in someone's day · an object-to-object chain (every cut is caused by an object on screen: laptop → terminal → blue field → dashboard → chat → chip → result card → dashboard → check → the board).
- **First two seconds:** the bench, the dead board, a phone lock screen "11:42 PM · Demo 9:00 AM", the title "11:42 pm." lands on the first beat.
- **Beats:** hook (the bench) → tension (the old way fails, in real error strings) → reveal (MCU Bridge, blue colour flood) → proof (name it, ask, compile, flash, verify — each one real UI) → payoff (the LED blinks, three times, on the beat) → claim → end card (cursor clicks the mark; a fill rises through it).
- **Look:** bench-mat grey, ink black, the product's blue rationed to the reveal, the result card and the end card.
- **Sound:** near silence while the old way fails; a minimal piano bed opens when the product appears; a soft click on every real click; the payoff plays with the bed held back.

## B — "The relay"
- **Device / form:** one object across apps (the sentence becomes a packet) · a flight through one world: a single long take that travels chat → cloud → PC → board without a cut.
- Beats: the packet is born in a chat box and keeps travelling; each stop is a verb; the camera never cuts.
- Look: product-white world with the blue packet as the only colour. Sound: a rising pulse that resolves on the LED.

## C — "Ask. Compile. Flash. Verify." (the bold one)
- **Device / form:** type carries the argument · cut on the beat: giant type and solid colour fields changing every half bar, the real UI only as one-second proofs.
- Beats: four verbs, four colour fields, a number lands ("127 tests pass"), the LED.
- Look: four flat colour planes from the product's own icon colours. Sound: a 120 bpm pulse.
